# AI Usage Statement

This project was developed with the assistance of AI-based tools, used strictly as learning and support aids, in accordance with the course guidelines.

## AI Tools Used
- ChatGPT (OpenAI)

## How AI Was Used

AI tools were used in the following ways:

- **Code clarification and learning**  
  To better understand Python, scikit-learn, and machine learning concepts (e.g. pipelines, preprocessing, evaluation metrics).

- **Debugging assistance**  
  To help identify and fix errors during development (e.g. shape mismatches, pipeline issues, train/test split problems).

- **Code refactoring suggestions**  
  To improve readability, simplify overly complex code, and make the project structure clearer and more consistent with course expectations.

- **Documentation support**  
  To help rewrite and clarify docstrings, comments, and documentation (README, report explanations).

## How AI Was NOT Used

- AI did **not** generate the project idea or research question.
- AI did **not** write the entire project autonomously.
- No code was included without being reviewed, understood, and adapted by the student.
- All modeling choices, feature engineering decisions, evaluation steps, and interpretations were made by the student.

## Student Responsibility

The student fully understands and can explain:
- the data preprocessing steps
- the feature construction and snapshot constraint
- the train/test split strategy
- the implemented models and evaluation metrics
- the results and their limitations

AI tools were used as an educational aid, similar to online documentation or forums, and not as a substitute for understanding or independent work.
